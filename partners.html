Partners & Impact

Aaj Ka Dhamaka is made possible through the generous support of corporate sponsors, campus partners, and community organizations. Their contributions help us deliver a high quality competition while also giving back to the broader community.

Community Impact Partner

Kiran

Kiran is a nonprofit organization dedicated to addressing and preventing domestic violence within South Asian communities across North Carolina. Kiran empowers survivors, raises awareness, and works to break stigma while promoting safety, healing, and long term resilience. Proceeds from Aaj Ka Dhamaka support Kiran and its mission.

Corporate Sponsors

NCAAT (North Carolina Asian Americans Together)

North Carolina Asian Americans Together (NCAAT) is a nonprofit organization dedicated to empowering and advocating for Asian American communities across North Carolina through civic engagement, leadership development, and grassroots organizing.

BUBBL’R

BUBBL’R is a sparkling antioxidant energy drink company designed for active and health conscious individuals. It is a cleaner alternative to traditional energy drinks and provides sustained energy without artificial sweeteners.

Wok On Fire

Wok On Fire is a returning sponsor of Aaj Ka Dhamaka, generously supporting the event by providing meals for participating dancers. Their continued partnership helps ensure performers are fueled and cared for throughout the competition.

Harris Teeter

Harris Teeter is supporting Aaj Ka Dhamaka by providing funding to purchase critical safety and wellness supplies for the event. Their contribution helps ensure a safe, well equipped environment for dancers before, during, and after performances.

Campus Support

UNC Student Government

UNC Student Government is providing funding to help facilitate AKD on Chapel Hill’s campus, enabling us to bring together students from universities across the country. Their support strengthens intercollegiate collaboration, fosters community engagement, and helps us execute the event at a high standard.
