Protocols

“Angel Shot”
  • Neat: escort to car
  • On Ice: call Lyft
  • With Lemon: in danger
CPR (quick steps)
  1. Shake and shout: “Are you ok?”
  2. Call for help: 911 + get AED.
  3. Check breathing/airway.
  4. Compress: ~100 BPM, 30 compressions + 2 rescue breaths.
  5. Follow AED instructions if available.
Bleeding / Sprains
  1. Assess injury.
  2. Bleeding: apply firm pressure with sterile cloth for ~10 min.
  3. Call SWA/EMT for help.
  4. Elevate above heart when possible.
  5. Do not remove soaked dressing; add more on top.
